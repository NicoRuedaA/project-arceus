# Texture mapping of species 1-150 (follow-up of the generation reviews and the exporter fixes)

Session texturas-150 (Claude Sonnet), 2026-10-18. Renders: CPU (lavapipe) through the engine's own glTF loader
(`render_gltf_headless`, 384 px, base pose) plus one in-game check (F7 spawner, Rattata). Blender 5.2 was used for the
source packs. Labels: VERIFICADO (checked by eye on before/after renders) / PROBABLE.

## Root cause (VERIFICADO)

The colour textures of most packs are addressed with the **horizontal UV axis doubled and a mirrored wrap**:
`u' = triangle_wave(2u)` - the left half of the UV space spans the whole texture and the right half is its mirror image.
The normal map (and the other maps of the same material) use the plain UV. The packs' own Blender graph samples the plain
UV for everything and the exporter copied the colour as is, so faces, bellies, ears, tongues and eyes landed on another part
of the texture (the "texture of another piece" seen in the reviews). The game engine was fine: the render of the converted
glTF in the engine and the original `.blend` in Blender show the same broken result; no Rust change was needed.
Three families, three sources of evidence:

1. **Trinity (`_alb`/`_lym`, `PokemonShader` group) packs - 77 packs.** The graph itself contains the answer:
   `UV -> Mapping(scale 2,1,1) -> modulo (2,1) -> mirror above 1 -> colour/mask textures` (the normal map has no such link).
   The exporter now detects that chain (`vector_chain_doubles_u`) and writes the baked colour as `[image | mirror]`.
2. **Let's Go / Sword-Shield (`_col`/`_amb`/`_emi`, plain Principled) packs and Gigantamax forms - 53 packs.** The graph holds no
   mapping, but the textures answer: sampling the texture on both sides of every UV seam gives colours that agree (error ~0)
   only with the right addressing (Rattata 0.09 -> 0.00, Omanyte 0.13 -> 0.00, Goldeen stays plain at 0.00). The test
   (`uv_mirror.mirrored_by_seams`) runs per material slot; eye slots that live in the left half of the UV space only (both
   eyes side by side in the texture) use the doubled addressing without a test. 2 packs (Paras) stay plain.
3. **PLA catalogue (`work/cache/pokemon_batch`, the models the game itself prefers) - 19 packs.** Same defect in the Rust batch
   export. Every half-width texture (`width * 2 == height`, 11 of them with a conclusive test, 0 counter-examples) and every
   square texture with a clear seam verdict (ratio < 0.3) is re-laid out in place (`.tools/fix_pla_uv_mirror.py`; originals in
   `work/cache/pokemon_batch_uv_orig/`, marker `.uv_mirror.json`, `--restore` undoes it). Tentacruel (73, ratio 0.45) was
   flipped wrongly at the first threshold 0.5, which is why the PLA tool uses 0.3.

Fix = re-lay out the colour texture (`[image | mirror]`, twice as wide): sampling it with the plain UV equals the doubled
mirrored addressing texel for texel, the mesh UVs and the normal map stay as they are (baking the UV instead made the
normal map show black patches).

## What is NOT fixed, and why

* **Species >= 151 have the same defect.** The tools are generic (`blender_export_pokemon.py` applies the rule to every pack,
  `fix_pla_uv_mirror.py --range 1-1025`); only 1-150 were reconverted here as asked. Reconvert with
  `python3 .tools/convert_national_batch.py --only 2,3,... --packs-file <list> --force --out-root work/cache/export_new` and
  swap the packs, as in `exportador.md`.
* **PLA catalogue regenerated by the Rust exporter brings the old textures back**: run `fix_pla_uv_mirror.py` again afterwards.
* **PLA Tentacruel (73), Kadabra (64), Machop-line / Rhydon (74, 111)**: the seam test is not conclusive (ratio 0.37-0.49), left
  untouched. PROBABLE that Rhydon's nose and a few details are still missing.
* **PLA eyes** are flat dark discs (no sclera/highlight): the baked eye colour of the Rust export, not a mapping problem.
* **Gigantamax Gengar** white box: an opaque helper mesh of the pack. **Butterfree** dark translucent halo behind the wings and
  Galarian Ponyta/Rapidash floating mane pieces: effect materials, not mapping. **Ekans** looks stretched in the rest pose
  (animation collapses it). **Paldean Tauros** (128_11/12/13_51) stays flat grey: Trinity colour layers without retail data.
* The decision rule for the `_col` family is statistical (seam test, thresholds 0.02 / 0.5); every verdict was checked by eye
  on the contact sheets, but a future pack with a flat texture could get an ambiguous verdict (it then keeps the plain UV).
* Verification in the real game: one F7 capture (Rattata). The other checks use the same Bevy glTF loader in the headless
  renderer; the in-game toon outline does not change UV addressing.
