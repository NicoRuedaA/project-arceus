# The two live abilities: Slow Start and Flower Gift (Brief D1)

Implements the only two abilities with an effect in this game's battle code, as found by the
phase-0 audit ([`abilities_held_items.md`](abilities_held_items.md)). Engine-side rules live in
`crates/pla/src/battle_engine/abilities.rs`; the game enables them for new sessions through
`BattleAssets`. Without `Battle::set_abilities` the engine behaves exactly as before.

## What was built

- `data/pml_personal_abilities.tsv` (new, generated by `.tools/decode_pml_tables.py`): the
  personal-table ability slots (fields 5, 6, 7) for all 1,276 rows; 318 rows present in the
  game (`is_active = 1`) carry non-zero slots.
- `BattleSpecies.abilities: [u16; 3]` and `Combatant.ability` (first slot; the engine assigns
  one ability per combatant, as the game does).
- `battle_engine/abilities.rs`: `AbilityRules { slow_start, flower_gift }` (`game_rules()` =
  both on, `default()` = off), `Battle::set_abilities`, `Battle::take_ability_events` and the
  two rules.
- Battle-log lines and `Event`-style notification of `SlowStartEnded` and
  `FormChangeRequested` (display text is port-side; the game's own strings are not decoded).
- `PLA_ABILITIES=off` (comparison switch) and `crates/pla/examples/ability_demo.rs`, a windowless
  run of the real session that prints the effect.

## Rules as implemented

| Rule | Behaviour | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Slow Start (112, Regigigas) | While a per-combatant counter is below 5 (counted from the first turn the Pokemon is on the field), its speed and the power of its physical moves are halved with the engine's Q12 multiply (round half down). The counter grows at the end of each turn and `SlowStartEnded` fires when it reaches 5 (normal from turn 6). | Duration and target PROBABLE (brief D1; the counter is compared with 4 in the disassembly); the count starts when the Pokemon enters the field is ASSUMED; the exact event ids are UNKNOWN. |
| Flower Gift (122, Cherrim 421) | With sun it requests form 1 (sunshine), otherwise form 0; the caller plays the model change. No stat change (the game code has no handler that would raise stats). | Form change VERIFIED (handler checks species 421 and sunny weather); "no stat change" PROBABLE. |

The weather that reaches a battle session is still `Unknown` in the port: wiring the world
weather (`game/weather.rs`) into `Battle::weather` remains open (listed as a Brief D1 risk,
depends on Brief 9). The rule can be exercised through the engine, which is what the tests and
the demo do.

## Tests (all green)

`cargo test -p pla --lib battle_engine::abilities`:

- `abilities_sheet_covers_present_species_and_the_two_live_abilities`: 318 present rows with a
  non-zero first slot; Regigigas `[112; 3]`, Cherrim `[122; 3]`, Pikachu `[9, 9, 31]`.
- `slow_start_halves_speed_for_the_first_five_turns`: rules off = Regigigas outspeeds Pikachu
  every turn; rules on = Pikachu first on turns 1-5, `SlowStartEnded` at the end of turn 5,
  Regigigas first on turn 6.
- `slow_start_halves_physical_power_only`: physical damage roughly halves (ratio 1.8-2.2 in the
  test), special damage is unchanged.
- `flower_gift_requests_cherrim_form_only_with_sun`: sun asks for form 1 once, rain asks back
  for form 0, rules off asks nothing.

Captures: `/home/nico/work/capturas_combate_d/d1_slow_start_flower_gift_abilities_on.log` and
`d1_slow_start_abilities_off_baseline.log` (same battle, same seed, `PLA_ABILITIES` on/off):
turns 1-5 deal ~24-26 damage on and ~46-54 off; turn 6 deals 55 on (the counter elapsed) and
the log shows "Slow Start wore off!".

## Unknown

- The exact meanings of the game's battle events `0x13`, `0x4c`, `0x93` and the counter limit
  (4 vs 5) — the implementation follows the brief's most probable reading.
- Whether the counter grows while the Pokemon is benched; the port only grows it while active.
- Which personal slot the game picks per Pokemon; the port uses the first slot (both live
  abilities repeat in all three slots of their species).
- The in-game trigger for Flower Gift with real world weather (needs the weather-to-battle
  wiring).
