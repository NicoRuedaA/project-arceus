# Terrain albedo: how the ground colour is composed (PLA update v262144)

## Finding (evidence-supported, not proven against the game's renderer)
The previous hypothesis (SplatColor0..3 <-> splat RGBA) was wrong in scope: the control texture has **8** channels.

- `areaNN_0_0_splat_alpha.bntx` is a BC7 **array of 2 layers** (BRTI array count 2, file size = 2 x 1 MiB + header), each 1024x1024. The material declares `ControlTextureNum = Two`. The old loader decoded only layer 0.
- Channels in order: layer0 R,G,B,A then layer1 R,G,B,A = weights of material slots `SplatColor0..7`. Area00 has 7 colour slots (channel 7 is ~0), areas 01 and 05 have 8.
- The 8 weights sum to ~255 per texel (1st..99th percentile 237..273 in area01, 242..268 in area00); they are therefore normalised by their sum when blending.
- Each slot references `ha_*_alb.bntx` (BC7 sRGB 256², ice 512²) and `_nrm.bntx` (BC5) that live in the same area pack; all were resolved by path hash (no unresolved slots in areas 00-06).
- `SplatST<k>` (and `...Far`, always equal) hold per-layer tiling vec4 `[sx, sy, 0, 0]`, stored immediately before the name's length word. Area00: 4,2.5,2.5,3,3,2,2.5; area01: 5,5,2.5,2.5,2.5,1,2.5,2.5; area05: 10,3,2,8,8,5,8,2. Other parameters: `SplatSTDisThreshold=10000` (far-switch distance), `SplatST<k>Threshold` 0.85-1.0, `Roughness*`; `TriplanarLayerCount` is the string "1" (area00) / "2" (area01), i.e. the first N layers (cliffs) are triplanar-projected. Shader name `Terrain`, material `TerrainMaterial`.
- The previous `terrain_*_normal` texture (BNTX `0x0e01`) is a 4-byte-per-texel format (file size equals the R32 heightmap's), not a 1-channel R16; the decoder's R16Unorm label for `0x0e01` is probably wrong (likely R16G16). Not needed for albedo; unverified.

## Evidence
1. Channels viewed as greyscale against the heightmap (area00): ch0 (cliff01) = steep contour bands plus the off-map/sea default; ch1 (grass01) = flat land; ch2 (grass02) = speckle plus blobs; ch4 (sand03) = one coastal patch at the shore; ch5/ch6 (gravel02/gravel01) = thin lines forming Jubilife's street grid and the roads out of town.
2. Placed Jubilife objects (`data/field_object_placements.tsv`, 240 in the town box): mean weight of gravel layers 5+6 within 6 texels of an object is 0.26, vs 0.15 over the town box and 0.02 over the whole area.
3. `.trins` trees vs layers (mean weight at the tree texel / area mean): area00 trees on grass01 0.69-0.76 / 0.40; area01 `plants_tree01` (852) on grass01 0.77 / 0.24, `plants_area01_tree01/02` on grass02+soil02+leaves01 (0.53+0.32+0.10); area05 trees and shrubs on snow01/snow02 (0.5/0.4 vs 0.25/0.27).
4. Area05 baked colour is snow white-blue with ice (blue) patches and dark mountain rock on ridges.

## Baked output
`cargo run -p pla --example terrain_albedo_bake -- --bin-root <bin> --area all --bake <repo>/work [--size 2048] [--mean]` writes `work/cache/terrain_albedo/areaNN.png` (git-ignored, 2048², sRGB). Blend in linear space, weights bilinearly upsampled, per-layer ground texture tiled at `SplatST / 4` repeats per metre (`DETAIL_TILE_DIVISOR` = 4.0 since the 2026-10-14 tiling fix, still an assumption); `--mean` uses each layer's mean colour instead (no tiling assumption).
Loader API: `pla::assets::terrain_albedo::{bake_albedo, albedo_cache_path, load_baked_albedo}`; `TerrainArea::splat_alpha1`, `TerrainMaterial::splat_tiling`, `Bntx decode_layer`.

To wire into the viewer (not done here; port_viewer.rs belongs to another agent): `let tex = load_baked_albedo(albedo_cache_path("work", area))?;` build a Bevy `Image` (Rgba8UnormSrgb) and set it as `base_color_texture` of the terrain material, with UVs = (x / extent, z / extent) over the whole 1025 m terrain (u along columns/x, v along rows/z, no flips), vertex colour white; fall back to `bake_albedo(&area, 2048, true)` when the cache file is missing.

## Looked at
Greyscale channels of areas 00, 01, 05 (montages), mean-colour and tiled bakes of 00, 01, 05, the Rust bake of 01 and 05, a 500 px crop of Jubilife. Area00: green meadows, dark cliff bands, sand at the coast, street grid and roads in grey-brown. Area05: snow white-blue, ice, dark rock. Area01: meadow, forest floor, red-brown soil patches, grey gravel/cliff.

## Unproven / ruled out
- Tiling period (`SplatST` meaning; the divisor 4.0 is still a guess); the in-game height/threshold-based blending (`SplatST<k>Threshold`, `Roughness*`), triplanar projection of cliffs, far/near switch, normal-map lighting and vegetation tint are NOT modelled. Colours are albedo only, no lighting.
- Large grey/dark-green regions in area01 and the left strip of area00 are the default/off-land layer 0; whether the game shows them is unknown.
- Detail masks `detailmap_N.trtbf` (4096² 0/255) meaning is still unknown (not examined here beyond the census).
- Ruled out: RGBA-only mapping to SplatColor0..3 (cannot explain 7-8 layers or the gravel streets); a second control file `splat_alpha1.bntx` (no such hash in any area pack).

## Ground layers, export and improved-texture bake (2026-10-12)
**Export** (`cargo run --release -p pla --example terrain_layers_export -- --bin-root <bin> --work <repo>/work`): every `SplatColor<k>` albedo and `SplatNormal<k>` normal of areas 00-06 as PNG in `work/cache/terrain_layers/<unique_name>.png` (git-ignored) plus the typed index `data/terrain_layer_index.tsv`. Dedupe key = texture name + FNV-1a hash of the decoded RGBA; same name with different content would get `__aNN` (none occurs: 91 unique textures = 46 albedo + 45 normal; areas 00 and 01 share `ha_area01_grass01/grass02/...`, area 06 reuses area 01/02 files; one texture may fill several slots, e.g. `ha_area05_snow02` in area 05). Sizes: 256² except `ha_area05_ice_alb` 512²; formats from the BNTX headers. Per-area columns: `slots`, `tiling` (SplatST x), `coverage_pct` (mean normalised splat weight over navmesh-walkable cells, 256² grid; area 06 has no navmesh so whole area), `all_pct`, `near_start_pct` (within 60 m of the `choose_safe_spawn` start, areas 00-05).

**Ranking** (`priority_rank`, albedo, areas 00-05): 0.5 x share of total weighted walkable m2 + 0.5 x share of near-start weight. Top 10: 1 `ha_area01_grass01_alb` (grass, areas 00+01; 63 % / 36 % of walkable land), 2 `ha_c_area02_grass01_alb` (grass, area 02, 48 %), 3 `ha_area03_sand01_alb` (sand, area 03, 12 % but 73 % at the start), 4 `ha_area05_snow01_alb` (snow, 34 %), 5 `ha_area05_snow02_alb` (snow, 36 %), 6 `ha_c_area04_glass02_alb` (grass, area 04, 44 %), 7 `ha_area01_grass02_alb` (grass), 8 `ha_area00_cliff01_alb` (cliff rock, 25 %), 9 `ha_area03_grass02_alb` (grass, 46 %), 10 `ha_area02_gravel02_alb` (gravel/roads). Plain names come from the texture-name tokens (`glass` is read as a typo of `grass`; the `c` in `ha_c_areaNN_*` is unexplained and ignored). Walkable-only coverage under-counts cliffs (steep ground is not walkable).

**Improved-texture bake**: `bake_albedo_with(area, &BakeOptions { size, detail, override_dir, threads })`. If `work/texturas_mejoradas/terrain_layers/<texture_name>__aNN.png` or `<texture_name>.png` exists (any size, PNG 8/16-bit grey/RGB/RGBA/palette) it replaces the original layer; the tiling period in metres is unchanged (`4 / SplatST`), so a larger texture gives more detail per metre. Each layer is converted to linear light once, mip-chained (u16), sampled trilinearly with wrap-around addressing (so no seam at tile edges; this also removes the old nearest-sampling aliasing), blended with the normalised splat weights and converted back to sRGB. CLI: `terrain_albedo_bake --bin-root <bin> --area N|all --bake <repo>/work [--size 4096] [--mean] [--overrides DIR] [--skip-if-unchanged]` (default size now 4096; `bake_albedo(area, size, detail)` keeps working with original layers only). The output stays `work/cache/terrain_albedo/areaNN.png`; the sidecar `areaNN.bake.json` records options, splat hash and per layer {slot, texture, source original/override, override file, size, hash, tiling}; `--skip-if-unchanged` skips when it equals the current inputs.
`./play.sh` runs `.tools/terrain_rebake.py` before building the overlay: an area is re-baked when it uses a layer with an improved file newer than its sidecar, when the sidecar is missing and an improved file exists, or when a listed improved file was deleted; areas without any improved file keep their existing bake; with `--area N` only that area is checked. Instructions for the user: `work/texturas_mejoradas/LEEME_SUELO.txt`.

**Verified**: 4 unit tests (override picked up incl. area-specific file and other-area isolation, wrap continuity in u and v, size option, sidecar skip/re-bake on new/changed file or size); real bake of area 01 at 4096² in 6 s (crop compared with the old 2048² bake: same layout, less aliasing noise); synthetic improved texture (1024² magenta) end-to-end in a scratch work root, sidecar skip and `terrain_rebake.py` dry-run/real run in a scratch root. **Not verified**: the viewer with a 4096² albedo or with improved textures (rule 7: viewer not run); area 06 start; the layer-normal maps were not used by the viewer at that time (now baked and applied, see the next section); the period `4/SplatST` is still a guess (4.0 since the 2026-10-14 tiling fix).

## Terrain normal map (relief) — `assets/terrain_normals.rs` (2026-10-13)
Mirrors the albedo bake for the ground **relief**: the material's `SplatNormal0..7` layers (BC5) are blended with the *same* eight normalised splat weights and the *same* `SplatST / 4` detail tiling into one tangent-space normal map per area, `work/cache/terrain_normals/areaNN.png` (RGB8, 2048²) plus the invalidation sidecar `areaNN.normals.json` (same skip-if-unchanged semantics as the albedo). BC5 has only two stored channels, so the tangent-space Z is rebuilt from R/G. The viewer sets `StandardMaterial::normal_map_texture` from the cache (a **linear** `Rgba8Unorm` image, never sRGB) and `build_mesh_chunks` now calls `Mesh::generate_tangents()` (mikktspace) so the chunks carry `ATTRIBUTE_TANGENT`; UVs are `(x / extent, z / extent)`, matching the bake.
CLI: `cargo run -p pla --example terrain_normals_bake -- --bin-root <bin> --area N|all --bake <repo>/work [--size 2048] [--mean] [--skip-if-unchanged]`.
Measured (debug, 2048², 3 worker threads): area00 1.6 s mean_z 0.9861 (tilted 33.7 %), area01 1.4 s mean_z 0.9938 (18.0 %), area02 1.7 s 0.9976 (6.9 %), area03 1.1 s 0.9906 (25.4 %), area04 1.5 s 0.9936 (21.0 %), area05 1.7 s 0.9988 (0.9 %), area06 0.9 s 0.9912 (31.2 %); `tilted` = share of pixels with reconstructed z < 0.99. The prior area test at 512² gave mean_z 0.9966/0.9987/0.9998 for 00/01/05.
- **Verified**: 6 unit tests (BC5 round-trip to unit length; a flat layer bakes to `(128,128,255)`; two opposite tilted layers cancel to a flat, unit-length blend; tile wrap continuity in u and v; sidecar skip/re-bake on changed layer content and on a different size) plus a `build_mesh_chunks` tangent test; the full `cargo test --offline -p pla --lib` suite (297 passed, 0 failed); real bakes of areas 00–06 (stats above, every decoded pixel unit length).
- **Assumed**: Z reconstruction `z = sqrt(max(0, 1 - x² - y²))`; blending as a weighted sum of unit normals then renormalised (no RNM); tangent-space +Y up (OpenGL); per-layer tiling equals the slot's `SplatST`. All are guesses, not decoded.
- **Unknown / not verified**: no render was run (rule 7) so the on-screen relief and the shader/lighting result are unjudged — the maintainer eyeballs it. The game's tangent-space Y convention is unknown (`StandardMaterial::flip_normal_map_y` is left `false`). Whether the original triplanar-projects the cliff layers (`TriplanarLayerCount`) is not modelled. The terrain's own `areaNN_0_0_normalmap` BNTX (`0x0e01`) is still unused and its format unverified.


## Real-time splat material — `game/terrain_splat.rs` + `terrain_splat.wgsl` (2026-10-14)
**Why.** The bake squeezes one ~1025 m area into 4096² (~0.25 m per pixel): a tiled layer ends up 16-50 px per tile on screen, a bigger improved layer only selects a coarser mip, the 1 px/m splat shows as blocky patches, and the top-down projection stretches rock on cliffs. The viewer now draws the ground from the layers on the GPU every frame (`--terrain splat`, the default); `--terrain bake` keeps the previous single-image path (weak PCs), and the splat path falls back to it automatically when an area has no usable splat data. The bake and `.tools/terrain_rebake.py` keep working: the bake is now the material's far-distance "macro" colour.

**How it hooks in.** `TerrainSplatPlugin` (registered in `port_viewer`) leaves the terrain spawn code alone: chunks are still spawned with the baked `StandardMaterial`; the plugin sees them (`TerrainChunkMarker`), builds the area's textures on a background thread (`build_splat_data`, ~0.35-0.42 s per area in release) and swaps each chunk's material to `ExtendedMaterial<StandardMaterial, TerrainSplatExt>` (one shared material per area; the base keeps the baked albedo, baked normal map and roughness, so PBR lighting, shadows, SSAO, fog and the art style keep working). Chunks, meshes and frustum culling are unchanged; a zone change repeats the swap for the new area and drops the old area's material.

**GPU inputs** (bindings 100-107): `texture_2d_array` of the 8 `SplatColor` layers, each resized on the CPU to one size (default 1024, capped at the largest source; box filter when shrinking, wrap-around) with a full mip chain built in linear light, stored `Rgba8UnormSrgb`, repeat addressing, trilinear + 16x anisotropic; improved PNGs from `work/texturas_mejoradas/terrain_layers/` win as in the bake (all 8 layers of areas 00-05 are currently improved 1024/1254 px files); the 8 `SplatNormal` layers (BC5, Z rebuilt) as a second array (256 px); the two splat control layers as two linear RGBA textures with mips, sampled bilinearly; a uniform with per-slot repeats per metre (`SplatST / tile_divisor`, default divisor 4.0 = the shared assumption), cliff mask, enabled mask, mean luminance and the tuning values.

**Shader.** Per fragment: splat weights (bilinear, plus a ±0.3 m noise warp of the lookup that breaks the 1 px/m steps) normalised by their sum; very steep faces (57°→68°) handed to the cliff slots; every layer with weight sampled with explicit gradients (anisotropic, mip-correct), triplanar on slopes (weights |n|⁸, projections under 2 % dropped, so ground under ~30° keeps one top-down sample) with whiteout-blended per-layer normals; a two-sample anti-tiling (noise-chosen UV offsets); a height-like blend (score = weight + 0.35 × the layer's luminance relative to its mean; layers within 0.25 of the best survive) so borders follow the texture; ±6 % low-frequency brightness variation; fade to the baked albedo between 70 and 220 m (no layer is sampled beyond that). All blending is in linear light. Debug views: `--terrain-debug weights|detail`.

**Cliff slots.** Matched by name (`cliff`, `mountain`, `rock`): areas 00 [0], 01 [0,1], 02 [0,1], 03 [0], 04 [0,1], 05 [0,1]. This agrees with the decoded `TriplanarLayerCount` of areas 00 (1) and 01 (2); the other areas' counts were not read.

**Knobs ready for the Graphics screen** (`TerrainSplatSettings` resource; live unless noted): `mode` (bake/splat), `layer_size` (rebuild), `max_layers` 1-8 (keeps the most covering slots), `triplanar`, `anti_tiling`, `layer_normals`, `anisotropy` (rebuild), `fade_start/end`, `height_strength`, `tile_divisor`, `debug_view`. CLI: `--terrain`, `--terrain-layer-size`, `--terrain-layers`, `--terrain-triplanar`, `--terrain-antitile`, `--terrain-normals layers|baked`, `--terrain-tile-divisor`, `--terrain-debug`.

**Verified.** 11 unit tests (`cargo test -p pla --lib terrain_splat`): mip count; layer array layout (8 layers × full chain, layer-major) and colour preservation through resize + mips in sRGB; improved layer wins and is capped by the layer size; wrap-around resampling keeps the mean (taps sum to 1 for 1254→1024); tiling math and the uniform (repeats, cliff/enabled masks, layer-count limit by coverage, divisor); splat weight normalisation, steep override and height blend (CPU mirrors of the shader); CLI flags; the shader's library part (everything but the Bevy-imported entry point) parses and validates with naga 29 including derivative uniformity; a headless Bevy app where terrain chunks get the shared splat material (2D-array view, sRGB, repeat + 16x anisotropy) and go back to the baked material with `--terrain bake`; an area without material keeps the bake; real area 01 builds 8 layers with cliff slots [0,1]. The viewer start-up guard runs `--terrain bake` and a cheap splat configuration. Windowless GPU captures (automation mode, release build, 1920×1080, day, sunny) of areas 00-05 at six cameras each — the five of `texturas_antes_despues/camaras.json` style (ground, rock, close, medium, far) plus a cliff face found by `terrain_splat_report` — were taken with `--terrain bake` and `--terrain splat` and looked at: improved layers are sharp near the camera, cliffs are no longer stretched, no seams between chunks were seen, the 70-220 m fade shows no ring and no obvious tiling far away. Images: `/home/nico/work/capturas_terreno/` (outside the repo).
